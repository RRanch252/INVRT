The Value of Compounding

We bring a systems thinking approach to every engagement, drawing on principles from natural design, multi generational family businesses, and quality focused organizations.

Capital Compounding

Quality businesses generate superior returns through durable competitive advantages that strengthen with age, pricing power that improves with reputation, and efficient capital allocation.

Relationship Compounding

The most valuable relationships compound through deepening trust, expanded opportunity access through network effects, and resilience during market disruptions.

Systems Value Creation

Systems oriented businesses create accelerating value through reinforcing feedback loops, network effects that increase with scale and time, and adaptability that converts challenges into growth opportunities.

Founder

Paul Higgins

With over two decades of experience across diverse industries, Paul brings a systems thinking approach to every client engagement. His background in quality focused business development has helped founders and business owners build enterprises designed for multi generational success.

Paul founded INVRT based on the belief that businesses can be designed to improve with time, creating compounding value for all stakeholders across generations.

Recognition

Paul is a contributor to Eric Markowitz's OUTLAST (Scribner, 2027). His writing on enduring enterprises has been highlighted by Stewart Brand and featured in Big Think's The Nightcrawler, and he is acknowledged in The Contemplative Leader by Patrick Boland and The Compounder's Element by Laurence Endersen.
