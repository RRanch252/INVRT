Questions

The questions we are working on.

Systems thinking is not a conclusion, it is a standing inquiry. These are the questions that guide the advisory work and the writing, drawn from real engagements and kept deliberately open. Where our own work speaks to a question, it sits underneath, one click away.

Start here · depth of leverage

Some questions move the system more than others.

Donella Meadows taught that the deepest places to intervene are the hardest to see. The questions are stacked by depth: parameters and tactics near the surface, paradigm at the floor, where the leverage is greatest. Hover a shallow question to see the deeper one that really governs it.
